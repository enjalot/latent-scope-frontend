Every method for projecting high-dimensional data into something we can
look at throws information away. The interesting question is not
*whether* it distorts the data, but *which* properties it tries to keep
and which it is willing to sacrifice.

This series works through the main families one at a time, organized by
what they preserve. The first article sets up the shared vocabulary;
the next nine each take one family.
